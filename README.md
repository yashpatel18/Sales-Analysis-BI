# 📊 ElectroHub Sales Dashboard (Power BI Project)

This Power BI project analyzes sales, profit, and discount trends for a fictional electronics retail business — **ElectroHub**. The dashboard provides insights across multiple business dimensions to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📦 Business Objectives

This project is designed to help ElectroHub’s management answer key business questions, including:

1. 📈 What are the top/bottom 5 products by Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold?
2. 🗓️ How do sales trends vary daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly?
3. 💸 What is the relationship between Sales and Profit?
4. 🆚 How do Sales/Profit/Quantity Sold compare across two selected time periods?
5. 🎯 What is the average discount offered in each discount category?
6. 🛒 How many total orders were placed?
7. 📂 Can we view key metrics like Sales, Profit, Discount, and Net Sales filtered by Product, Date, Customer ID, or Promotion Category?
8. 🌆 What are the city-wise sales figures?

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

- File: `Store Data.xlsx`
- Source: Provided for internal BI practice
- Key Columns: Order ID, Customer ID, Product Name, Date, Quantity, Price, Profit, Discount, Category, City

## 🎨 Dashboard Features

- Fully interactive Power BI dashboard
- Time-based slicers and filters
- Drill-down and tooltip interactivity
- Comparison visualizations for any two periods
- KPIs and card visuals summarizing performance
